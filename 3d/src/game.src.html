How to play

Every rider's toot pushes the raft the other way.

The raft keeps spinning, so watch who is at the back.

Spikes pop a heart. Soft green walls are safe to bump.

Grab beans to refill gas. Reach the finish fast.

Toot the same pig 3 times fast for a ROCKET TOOT.

Tap a rider or the buttons · 1–4 toot that rider
Arrows/WASD or tap the water: best rider toots that way
